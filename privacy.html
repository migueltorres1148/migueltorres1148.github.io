Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, Miguel Torres, to let the open-source tool rclone access his own Google Drive from his own computers.

  • The only account that authorizes this client is the owner's own Google account.
  • Google Drive data is read and written only on the owner's own machines, by rclone, at the owner's direction.
  • No data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with any third party by the operator of this page.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party access page.
